Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

KAITS is school software. Schools use it to run attendance, the parent feed, pickup, fees and messages. This page explains what information KAITS handles, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. KAITS is run by KAITS, an MSME registered in India (Udyam UDYAM-UP-28-0250243).

Who decides what is collected

Your school decides what it records about its students, parents and staff, and KAITS keeps and processes that information for the school. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the school is the data fiduciary and KAITS acts on its behalf. For most requests about a child's records, the school office is the right first stop.

What KAITS holds

  • Parents and guardians: name, mobile number, email if given, relationship to the child, and the people you allow to pick up your child.
  • Students: what the school records, such as name, class, attendance, the daily diary, photos the school shares with you, and fee records.
  • Staff: name, mobile number, role and branch.
  • Payments: amounts, dates and receipts. Card and UPI details are handled by the payment gateway (Razorpay or Cashfree) and never reach KAITS.
  • Sign-in: the mobile number you sign in with and when you signed in, kept to protect your account.

WhatsApp

  • We send you only the kinds of updates your school has turned on, such as attendance, pickup, the diary and fee reminders.
  • When you sign in by sending a LOGIN message, we use your number and the code in it only to confirm it is you.
  • Other messages you send to the number are passed to your school so staff can reply, and kept with your school's messages.
  • We do not use WhatsApp messages for advertising and do not share them with anyone other than your school and Meta.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent personal information.
  • Show advertising to parents or students.
  • Share one school's information with another school.

Where it is kept and for how long

Information is stored securely and access is limited to the school's own staff and the few KAITS people who keep the service running. We keep it while the school uses KAITS. When a school leaves, its information is deleted within 90 days unless the law requires us to keep part of it, such as fee receipts.
