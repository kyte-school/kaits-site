Who it's for

Built for the four people who run a school.

A parent opens the app to answer one question: what do I need to know or do today? A teacher has forty children and no time. The office lives on a keyboard. The owner wants to know if the money came in. KAITS gives each of them their own screen.

Parents

Phone app and WhatsApp

  • A daily feed per child: attendance, diary, homework, photos, notices.
  • Pay fees by UPI in two taps; receipts and the full ledger in one place.
  • Apply for leave, message the class teacher, add who may pick up.
  • Brothers and sisters in different schools show up under one login.

Teachers

Phone app that works offline

  • Attendance on a tap grid that saves even without a signal.
  • The preschool diary: mood, meals and nap for the whole class at once.
  • Posts with photos, parent messages and leave approvals.
  • The pickup board at dismissal, with health flags on the roster.

The office

Web, built for the keyboard

  • Admissions from enquiry to enrolment, with an online form for the website.
  • A seven-step admission that links siblings and collects documents.
  • Fee counter, dues and defaulters, concessions and Tally export.
  • Excel import and export on every list.

Owners and principals

Dashboard on web and phone

  • Collection, dues and admissions across every branch.
  • Approvals for reversals, concessions and exceptions in one queue.
  • Custom class sections, fee plans and late fee rules per branch.
  • An audit trail of every money change and who made it.

Fees

The fee office, without the register.

Every rupee is a receipt, and every receipt is on the ledger. Nothing is edited or deleted; corrections are entries of their own, so the books always add up.

Collect at the counter in secondsCash, cheque, UPI or bank transfer, with gap-free receipt numbers for each financial year.
Scan a cheque or a UPI screenshotKAITS reads the amount, cheque number, bank and UTR and fills in the form. It warns when the words and figures don't match.
Send a payment linkThe family pays by UPI, card or net banking from WhatsApp. A parent at the desk can scan a QR code instead.
Paid straight to the schoolOnline payments go through Razorpay or Cashfree into the school's own account, never through ours. Receipts are issued the moment the money lands.
Reminders, late fees and refundsReminders a week before, on the day and after. Late fee rules with a grace period and a cap. Refunds go back through the gateway, in full or in part.

Safe pickup

Every child goes home with the right person.

Parents list who may collect their child, with a photo. A one-off pickup is added from the app and lasts the day.

Teachers hand over from the board at the gate and see at once when someone isn't on the list.

The parent gets a WhatsApp message the moment the handover is marked, with who collected the child and when.

Trust

Children's data and school money, handled with care.

Schools hold some of the most sensitive data there is. KAITS is built so that the safe way is the only way.

Your money, your account

Fees settle directly into the school's bank account through its own gateway. KAITS never holds fee money.

Books that can't be quietly changed

The ledger only grows. Reversals, refunds and concessions are new entries with a reason and a name attached.

Each school sealed off

Every school's records are kept apart at the database itself, not just in the app. Staff see only their classes and branches.

Ready for India's data law

Privacy notice and photo consent on a parent's first sign-in, built for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Approvals where it matters

Reversing a payment or waiving a fee can need the principal's sign-off, with everything kept in the audit log.

Made for real phones

Designed for low-cost Android phones on patchy 4G, in English and Hindi, with more languages to come.
